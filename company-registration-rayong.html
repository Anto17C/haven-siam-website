Rayong is where we are based, so for businesses here we can meet in person, visit premises and help with the local steps that are hard to do from abroad.

Rayong sits on the Eastern Seaboard, with industrial estates, ports, beach towns such as Ban Phe and the departure point for Koh Samet. That mix means very different businesses start here: factories and trading companies alongside guesthouses, bars, clinics and laundromats. Most foreign-owned companies register as a Thai Limited Company, and the right structure depends on what the business does and who owns it. We are not a law firm; registration is coordinated with licensed Thai professionals while we manage the timeline.
