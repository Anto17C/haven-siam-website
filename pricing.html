Your Quote

How a Quote Is Built

Every quote separates four kinds of cost, so you can see what you pay Haven Siam and what goes to others.

Haven Siam Fee

The package range above, set by your agreed scope, plus any content add-on itemized separately.

Licensed Partner Fees

Fees charged by Walailak Law Firm for legal and visa work, and by the accounting firm for your books. Itemized separately in the quote.

Government Fees and VAT

Filing and registration fees paid to the authorities, plus VAT where it applies. Itemized separately in the quote.

Recurring Costs

First-year domain, hosting and business email are included in the setup price. Renewals after the first year are coordinated by Haven Siam and itemized separately, apart from any monthly content support.

With your quote we give you a realistic timeline for each stage.

Prices shown are Haven Siam's digital build and support fees only. Partner fees, government fees, and renewals such as domain and email are itemized separately in your quote.

Prices are in Thai baht (THB). The final price and payment currency will be confirmed in your quotation.
